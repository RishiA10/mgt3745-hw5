# PROJECT

Status: ACTIVE.

## Problem

Competitive dance team members balance late practices with academic responsibilities such as coursework, exams, interview preparation, and MCAT preparation. Practices can run later than expected, reducing the time members have available for studying and sleep even when they plan their academic work in advance.

## Users

The primary users are competitive dance team members who need to coordinate practice commitments with different academic obligations. Interviews showed that members already plan work ahead of time and communicate important conflicts to team leadership, but unexpectedly long practices and different academic demands can still create scheduling conflicts.

## Project Scope

The project helps members communicate when they are unavailable for practice because of academic or other commitments. Members can submit unavailable dates and times, view saved availability, and check a proposed practice time against submitted availability to identify conflicts.

The project supports scheduling and communication. It does not decide whether a member should attend practice, automatically change team schedules, or replace communication between members and team leadership.

## Evidence

The project framing is based on the two exploratory interviews documented in [USERS.md](USERS.md). Those interviews support the existence of scheduling conflicts for the interviewed participants but do not establish how common the problem is across all competitive dance teams.
